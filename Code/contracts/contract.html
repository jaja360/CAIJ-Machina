Exclusive Supply Agreement

Between Lex Veille and PDrago inc.

This Exclusive Supply Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of [Effective Date], by and between:

Lex Veille, a company duly organized and existing under the applicable laws, with its principal place of business at [Lex Veille Address] ("Supplier"); and

PDrago inc., a company duly organized and existing under the applicable laws, with its principal place of business at [PDrago inc. Address] ("Client").

1. Purpose

The purpose of this Agreement is to establish the terms under which Supplier shall exclusively provide and sell pest control chemicals to Client for Client's commercial and operational use.

2. Exclusivity

Supplier shall be the sole and exclusive provider of pest control chemicals to Client for a period of ten (10) years beginning on the Effective Date. During this term, Client shall not purchase pest control chemicals from any other provider unless Supplier is unable to supply the requested products within commercially reasonable timelines and quantities.

3. Products

The products covered by this Agreement include pest control chemicals, treatments, and related chemical formulations required by Client for pest management activities, as may be listed in purchase orders, product schedules, or written addenda agreed to by both parties.

4. Term

This Agreement shall remain in force for ten (10) years from the Effective Date, unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Agreement.

5. Pricing and Payment

Pricing for products supplied under this Agreement shall be set out in quotations, schedules, or purchase orders accepted by both parties. Client shall pay all undisputed invoices within thirty (30) days of invoice date unless otherwise agreed in writing.

6. Delivery and Compliance

Supplier shall deliver the pest control chemicals in accordance with agreed delivery schedules and applicable laws and regulations governing the manufacture, packaging, labeling, transport, and sale of such chemicals. Client shall use, store, and handle all products in compliance with applicable law and safety requirements.

7. Warranties

Supplier warrants that the pest control chemicals supplied under this Agreement shall conform to applicable specifications and legal requirements at the time of delivery. Except as expressly stated in this Agreement, no other warranties are made, whether express or implied, except those that cannot legally be excluded.

8. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement upon material breach by the other party if such breach remains uncured for thirty (30) days after written notice. Either party may also terminate this Agreement if the other party becomes insolvent, enters bankruptcy proceedings, or ceases operations.

9. Confidentiality

Each party agrees to keep confidential any non-public business, technical, pricing, or operational information disclosed in connection with this Agreement, except where disclosure is required by law or authorized in writing by the disclosing party.

10. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of [Jurisdiction], without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

11. Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties regarding its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions, understandings, and agreements, whether oral or written.
